# Season 11 OV Racing Championship — official race results

This repository hosts the **official, unedited Season 11 race results** for the OV Racing Championship,
generated by **Race Grok**, the championship's race simulator.

Live site: https://ovracingchampionship.github.io/race-results/

## Why the commit history matters

Every race result, standings update and race page is published here by Race Grok as it is generated.
The **commit history is the proof**: each change is timestamped and permanent, so anyone can check that
no result was edited after it was posted. History is never rewritten — if a duplicate or empty commit
ever appears, it stays in place and is explained rather than removed.

## What's here

- `index.html` — home page: confirmed Season 11 groups, standings and race results (standings fill in after the first race)
- `data/results.json` — machine-readable season data the pages are built from
- `img/` — cover photo, championship logo, and the confirmed group-draw graphic
- `fonts/` — self-hosted fonts (SIL Open Font License, see `fonts/LICENSES.txt`)

The 7 groups of 4 are confirmed (28 drivers). Race pages (`races/`) are added as races are run, starting the week of Nov 9, 2026.
